System capabilities

One plan for flexible, interoperable communications.

Arcadia Command Solutions operates on licensed radio frequencies with equipment offering a wide range of digital and analog capabilities. That flexibility allows channel plans and equipment assignments to be tailored to different user groups and integrated into a broader interoperability plan with participating agencies. Repeater and mobile-radio options can extend coverage and support a dedicated communications station at the command post.

  • DMR and analog portable radios
  • Licensed UHF frequencies
  • Repeater-capable systems
  • Mobile-radio communications stations
  • Radio programming and configuration
  • Motorola, Vertex Standard, and Retevis equipment

Preplanning

Interoperability starts before the event.

We coordinate with the governmental and nongovernmental organizations involved in the operation to determine how information must move between teams, agencies, and command.

01

Understand the operationReview the site, operating area, staffing, assignments, critical functions, expected radio traffic, and known coverage concerns.

02

Coordinate the partnersWork with participating public-safety agencies, government organizations, event staff, contractors, and volunteer groups to identify interoperable communications needs.

03

Build the planDevelop channel assignments, radio identifiers, communications procedures, contingencies, and a complete incident communications plan.

04

Prepare the systemProgram, label, stage, deliver, test, and issue equipment so users know what they have and how to use it.
