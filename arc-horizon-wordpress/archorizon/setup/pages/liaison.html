Liaison

Keep partner agencies in the incident picture. Liaison records who is involved, what each agency needs, and the coordination that produces action.

In this workspace

What the section handles.

Agency board

See participating agencies, their current status, contacts, and involvement.

Contact log

Record conversations, commitments, and follow-up with a clear time and source.

Feedback to action

Turn partner feedback into tasks or strategies when it changes the plan.

Planning connection

Keep interagency meetings and coordination aligned with the planning cycle.

In practice

From input to action.

The workspace shares its records with related areas of the incident, so an update can inform the next task or decision.

  1. ConnectIdentify the partner and record the point of contact.
  2. CoordinateLog requests, constraints, and commitments.
  3. Follow throughRoute actionable items to planning or operations and track the outcome.
