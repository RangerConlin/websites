Support & safety

Weather

Bring weather into planning and safety decisions. The weather workspace presents the incident location’s conditions alongside forecast and aviation information.

In this workspace

What the section handles.

Incident summary

Review current conditions, a three-day outlook, and active alerts for the incident area.

Aviation weather

Read METAR and TAF information for flight operations.

NWS alerts

See National Weather Service warnings and advisories relevant to the operation.

Location control

Use the ICP location or an override when planning for another area.

In practice

From input to action.

The workspace shares its records with related areas of the incident, so an update can inform the next task or decision.

  1. ReviewCheck current and forecast conditions for the operating area.
  2. ApplyConnect a weather concern to assignment timing, aviation work, or a safety trigger.
  3. UpdateRefresh information when connectivity is available and conditions change.
