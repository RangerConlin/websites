Finance

Give incident leadership a practical view of expected resource costs. Finance draws on resource use and fuel assumptions to support planning conversations.

In this workspace

What the section handles.

Cost forecast

Estimate how resource decisions affect the incident’s expected cost.

Fuel profiles

Use a consistent set of gas, diesel, Jet-A, and 100LL assumptions with local averages.

Resource context

Relate cost inputs to vehicles, aircraft, equipment, and staffing.

Planning handoff

Share forecast context with command and logistics as the operation changes.

In practice

From input to action.

The workspace shares its records with related areas of the incident, so an update can inform the next task or decision.

  1. Set assumptionsEnter current local rates and resource profiles.
  2. EstimateReview the projected cost of the operational plan.
  3. ReviseUpdate the forecast when resources or expected duration change.
